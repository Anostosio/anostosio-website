CONTEXT / PROBLEM

Context often sounds more certain than it is°

Brand projects begin with scattered notes, preferences and unverified assumptions. A generic AI response can polish that material until the gaps disappear. The document looks complete, but it is difficult to trust or use for real decisions.

IDEA / SOLUTION

Make uncertainty visible°

The product collects context through a project-aware questionnaire, scores input readiness locally and produces 13 editable sections. Every section carries an evidence status and source fields. It also offers two deliberately different strategic routes as proposals for discussion, never as research findings.

PRODUCT

What the product does°

01

13 SECTIONS

Positioning, value proposition, messages, visual direction, risks and ordered next steps.

02

READINESS

A five-dimension local diagnostic improves the input before an AI request is spent.

03

EVIDENCE MAP

Grounded, mixed, hypothesis and needs-validation states expose the quality of support.

04

TWO ROUTES

Two intentionally different strategic routes with advantages and risks.

05

RECOVERABLE

If the API fails, answers remain intact; the user can retry or create an explicitly labelled local draft.

06

EXPORT

Editing, eight-item local history, clipboard, JSON import/export and a print-ready A4 document.

UX / UI

The UX is built around trust, not magic°

The central decision is to expose the model’s limits. The interface shows where each conclusion came from, preserves work through failure and distinguishes AI output from local fallback. A user can review, edit and export the result without creating an account.

AI / HUMAN

Anastasia defined the brief methodology, product structure, UX and quality rules. AI supported the design and development process; inside the product it acts as a controlled generator for a strictly structured result.
