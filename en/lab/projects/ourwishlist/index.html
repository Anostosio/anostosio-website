CONSUMER PRODUCT / TELEGRAM MINI APP

OurWishList°

A private wishlist for couples inside Telegram — from a product link to a hidden gift reservation.

CONTEXT / PROBLEM

A couple’s wishes get scattered across chats, notes and links°

Ideas disappear, details need to be asked again and the same gift can be bought twice. A public wishlist does not address the privacy and surprise mechanics of a couple.

IDEA / SOLUTION

One private space inside Telegram°

Two people connect through a single-use invite and maintain personal and shared lists. Wishes can be entered manually or by URL; a partner can reserve a gift without revealing it to the author.

PRODUCT

The couple workflow°

01

PRIVATE PAIR

One active pair of no more than two people with a single-use invite.

02

FOUR LISTS

Mine, Partner, Shared and Fulfilled in one Mini App.

03

ADD BY URL

Title, image and price are read from JSON-LD, Open Graph and metadata.

04

MANUAL FALLBACK

A wish can still be edited and saved when a store blocks metadata.

05

HIDDEN RESERVATION

A partner reserves a gift without changing what its author sees.

06

SEARCH & FILTERS

Category, budget, priority, date and text search.

UX / UI

The mobile gallery comes first°

The Mini App is designed for Telegram at 360–480 px. Lists open directly, filters stay out of the gallery, primary actions remain thumb-friendly, and light or dark theme is remembered.

AI / HUMAN

AI assisted the build; product decisions stayed human°

AI supported product design and development. The shipped product does not generate gift recommendations or send private wishes to an LLM.

RESULT

A working Telegram product for two people°

MVP 0.2 is deployed on Vercel. Pairing, wish creation, editing, archive, hidden reservations and PostgreSQL persistence are covered by the implementation and tests. Public feeds, AI recommendations and groups larger than two are outside the MVP.
