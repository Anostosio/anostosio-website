/02

What the site offers°

The website presents a portfolio, service descriptions and indicative starting prices for design work. The pricing page is intended for preliminary orientation and to invite a discussion of a specific task.

Prices are expressly stated as starting prices. The final scope, price, timing, revision rounds, source materials and deliverables depend on the particular project.

Viewing a price, completing the brief, creating an email or clicking “Discuss a project” does not itself create an online order or automatically conclude a contract.

The terms of any actual project are agreed separately before work starts.

/03

Brief & enquiries°

The brief does not submit data to an Anostosio° server. It locally prepares an email and opens the visitor's mail application through a mailto: link. The visitor independently decides whether to send the message.

Once an email is actually sent, the owner receives it and uses its contents to reply, estimate the task and discuss a possible project. See the Personal Data Policy for details.

/04

Payment & agreement°

The website has no checkout, online cash register, payment form, customer account or technical mechanism for immediate contract acceptance. It does not charge cards or collect payment credentials.

If both sides later decide to work together, the form of the agreement and payment procedure are determined separately, taking account of the service provider's actual legal/tax status and applicable law at the time.

Publication of starting prices should not be understood as an unconditional promise to perform any task at that price without prior agreement.

/05

Intellectual property°

Unless a project states otherwise, the website structure, original Anostosio° copy, visual design and portfolio materials are protected under applicable intellectual-property law.

Showing a client, educational or conceptual project does not grant website visitors rights to third-party trademarks, photographs, text or other materials appearing in that case study.

If you believe a particular item infringes your rights, email Anostosio@yandex.ru and identify the page and basis of the request.

/06

External services°

The website includes ordinary links to third-party resources such as Telegram, VK and HeadHunter. After following such a link, the visitor interacts with that service under its own rules.
