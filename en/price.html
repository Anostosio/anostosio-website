BEFORE WE START

Frequently asked questions°

01 How can I get an exact quote?

Complete the short brief with the task, scope, timing and available materials. Once I review the details, I can provide a quote and suggest the most suitable format.

02 How many revisions are included?

The project includes 2 revision rounds. Further revisions are charged separately.

03 What should the client prepare?

A description of the task, final text, dimensions, logos, photos and other source materials. References and anti-references help define the visual direction faster.

04 Which file formats will I receive?

The set depends on the project: print-ready layouts, publication images, presentation files or vectors. We agree on the required formats and source files before the project starts.

05 Can a project be completed urgently?

Yes, when there is an available slot. The rush fee is 50–100%, depending on scope and timing.

06 How does payment work?

The price, stages and payment schedule are agreed and confirmed before work begins, so the terms are clear to both sides.
