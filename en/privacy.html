/01

Scope°

This policy describes personal data processing connected with https://anostosio.ru/ and with people who voluntarily contact the operator about design services.

Personal data operator: Anastasia Vasilievna Kravtsova, an individual. Privacy requests can be sent to Anostosio@yandex.ru.

The policy is based on the actual website architecture and on Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ on Personal Data. It does not mean that every item listed below is collected from every visitor.

Please do not send passport details, health information, biometric data, payment-card details or other sensitive information through the brief or an ordinary email unless there is a genuine need to do so.
/02

How the site works°

Anostosio° is a static website. Brief fields are filled in inside the visitor's browser. The website has no server-side form handler and does not send the brief into its own database.

When “Create email” is used, the website builds a mailto: link and opens the visitor's mail application. The operator does not receive those answers until the visitor actually sends the email. “Copy answers” only copies text to the visitor's clipboard.

/03

Data involved°

Enquiries and the brief

Depending on what the visitor chooses to provide: name or project name, reply contact, requested service, project description, target audience, timing, budget range, links to materials and references, additional details and, after an email is sent, the sender address, date/time and standard email metadata.

Yandex Metrica

Only after the visitor chooses “Accept”, the site loads Yandex Metrica counter 110819448. Under Yandex's current terms, this may involve page activity, cookies and browser identifiers, device and operating-system data and other technical information required to produce visit statistics.

Session Replay (Webvisor) is disabled in the website configuration. The site does not intentionally send brief contents, names or email addresses to Metrica as visit or user parameters.

Page delivery

The hosting infrastructure may technically receive standard network information such as IP address, request time, requested URL, referrer and user-agent in order to deliver and protect the site.

/05

Cookies & analytics°

Yandex Metrica is not loaded before consent. “Accept” and “Decline” are both available, and the analytics choice is stored locally so it does not have to be requested on every page.

The website itself uses localStorage for anostosio_language (language preference) and anostosio_analytics_consent_v1 (analytics choice plus technical version/time information).

After opt-in, Yandex Metrica may set its own cookies and localStorage identifiers. Their current list and retention periods are determined by Yandex documentation.

The choice can be changed at any time using the “Cookies” control. On refusal, the site sets Yandex's documented counter-disable flag, stops an already running counter on the current page and removes Metrica first-party cookies/localStorage that are accessible to the site. Cookies created directly on Yandex domains cannot be removed by JavaScript running on anostosio.ru.

/06

Services & recipients°

Yandex LLC — Yandex Metrica

When analytics is enabled, Yandex processes visitor data on behalf of the operator within the scope described in the current Metrica terms. Analytics is not loaded before opt-in.

Yandex Mail

Emails voluntarily sent to Anostosio@yandex.ru pass through Yandex's email infrastructure under its own terms and privacy documentation.

Hosting provider

The provider used to publish the website may receive network information required to deliver pages and protect infrastructure. The operator documents the actual hosting location and settings separately.

External links

The website links to services such as Telegram, VK and HeadHunter. It does not embed those services in an iframe or send them the brief content before an ordinary link is followed.

/07

Retention°

Enquiry data is kept no longer than necessary to reply, discuss and potentially carry out a project. If no agreement is reached, ordinary business correspondence may be retained for up to one year after the last substantive contact unless longer retention is reasonably necessary to protect legal rights or comply with law.

If an agreement or payment relationship follows, related correspondence and documents may be retained for the periods reasonably required to perform obligations, protect the parties' rights and comply with applicable legal duties.

Language and consent settings stay in the browser until changed, cleared by the visitor or removed by the browser. Metrica identifier lifetimes are determined by Yandex settings and documentation.

/08

Your rights°

A data subject may request information about processing and, where the law provides, ask for correction, blocking or deletion, or withdraw consent where a specific processing activity is based on consent.

Requests can be sent to Anostosio@yandex.ru. The request should contain enough information to identify which data it concerns. The analytics opt-in can be withdrawn directly on the site through the “Cookies” control.

/09

Data minimisation°

The website has no server-side visitor database, does not save brief fields to localStorage, does not load Metrica before opt-in and does not use Webvisor.

If the architecture changes — for example by adding a server form, CRM, online payment, advertising pixels or new external services — the policy and internal data map must be reviewed before the new feature is launched.

/10

Updates°

The current version is published on this page. Material changes to data categories, purposes or services are reflected here when the relevant functionality changes.
